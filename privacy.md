# Bear River: privacy policy

**Effective:** November 11, 2026

Bear River is a river and creek companion for fly anglers and paddlers. You buy it once. There is no account, no
sign-in, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your phone

Your logbook: catches, trips and their GPS tracks, photos (saved without the camera's location data), hazards you
mark, gear, fly boxes, camps, alerts, saved maps and settings. Bear River has no account to attach them to and no
server that receives them. They are part of your phone's own backup if you use one. You can copy the logbook out
yourself, and bring a copy in, from Basecamp › Settings › Logbook backup.

## What leaves your phone

**Requests for river data, weather and maps.** To show flows, forecasts, access points and map layers, the app asks
public services for them. Each request carries the place being asked about (a gauge, the part of the map on screen,
a camp's location, a point you press on the map, or the area around you when you search for water nearby, rounded
to about 10 km for a name search) and, as with any website, your network address. No name, account or device
identifier is attached. The services are: the U.S. Geological Survey, the National Weather Service and NOAA, the
Pennsylvania Fish & Boat Commission, PASDA, the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers' National Inventory of Dams, the PAD-US
public-lands map (hosted by Esri), the Iowa Environmental Mesonet (radar and rain layers), GitHub (Bear River's
fishing-report feed and state map packs) and Apple Maps.

**The guides, Brookie and Eddy.** When you ask a guide a question, your question and what the guide looks up in the
app to answer it are sent to Anthropic's Claude through Bear River's server (a Cloudflare Worker). That can include
your saved waters and their current conditions, the water you are nearest, your logbook's trips, catches and notes
when the guide reads it, and the name and dates of a camp you send a guide to scout. Anthropic processes this under
its own privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). If you never ask a guide anything, none of this is sent.

**What Bear River's server keeps.** None of the conversation. To hold each phone to its daily allowance, it keeps,
for two days, a count against a random identifier that your copy of the app made for itself, and a count against
your network address. The identifier is not tied to your name, your Apple ID or anything else about you, and it is
replaced if you reinstall the app.

## Location

Bear River uses your location to find the nearest river, to place your catches and hazards, and to record a trip's
track while a trip you started is running (including with the screen off, which is why iOS may show that the app is
using your location in the background). Your location is used on the phone. It leaves the phone only as described
above: as the area a request is about, and in a guide's answer when you ask one.

## Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you share it. A trip's share card is an image you choose to send; privacy zones you set
(Basecamp › Settings › Location privacy) are cut from its map, and on sensitive waters the card starts with its map
off.

## Children

Bear River is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with its date.
