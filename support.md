# Bear River: support

## Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/bagelboy137/bear-river-feed/issues, Bear River's public repository. Say which river or screen the question is about and, if something looks wrong, what the screen
said.

## Common questions

**The readings say "saved" or look old.** Bear River shows the last copy it downloaded when there is no signal. Open
the app once with signal before you lose it (the motel, the last gas stop) and it saves a fresh copy for the river.

**"USGS is limiting requests from this network."** USGS allows each network a set number of requests an hour, and
phones on one carrier or Wi-Fi can share it. The app waits and tries again by itself; the saved readings stay on
screen with their time.

**A paddle level says "Est."** The range for that river is a starting estimate, not one confirmed by local
paddlers. Treat it as a first look and check the river yourself.

**A river says "Short record".** USGS has under ten years of daily flows for that gauge, too few to say what is
normal for the date, so the app doesn't.

**My trip shows "Not recording: location is off".** Bear River needs Location access to record a track. The trip
sheet has a button that opens Settings.

**A guide says it has answered a lot today.** Each phone gets 30 questions a day and 100 a month. The count resets
by the phone's own calendar.

**Moving to a new phone, or keeping a copy of my logbook.** Basecamp › Settings › Logbook backup. "Back up the
logbook" makes a folder you can save to Files or AirDrop; "Bring in a backup" adds whatever is in a backup that the
phone doesn't already have.

**Put-ins and campsites are missing for my state.** They come from OpenStreetMap, and Bear River carries that data
for Pennsylvania, New Jersey, New York, Delaware, Maryland and Virginia so far. Gauges, river lines, search and the
stream card work in every state.

**Refunds.** Purchases are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
